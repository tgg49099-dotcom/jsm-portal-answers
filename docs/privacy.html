Privacy policy — JSM Portal Answers

Last updated: 18 November 2026.  ·  Applies to the Forge app JSM Portal Answers and to the two Atlassian MCP tools it exposes.

1. What the app is

JSM Portal Answers is a Jira Service Management (JSM) Forge app that exposes two read-only tools to the Atlassian MCP server:

  • list_portal_request_types — what a customer can ask for on a customer portal;
  • describe_request_type_form — the fields of the create form of a request type.

It declares a single permission scope, read:servicedesk-request, and it never writes anything.

2. Stores or transmits personal data?

No. The app has no storage (no storage, no key-value store, no entities), writes nothing, and sends nothing to the developer, to a third party or to any external service. No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies.

3. What data does it read?

Only the metadata of the JSM customer portal of the site where the app is installed:

  • the customer portals of the site (service desk id, project key, project name);
  • the categories of a portal (request type groups);
  • the request types: name, description, help text, category, restriction status, and whether the person asking may raise them;
  • the fields of the create form of a request type: label, required or not, type, help text, possible values, default values.

It reads no issue, no comment, no attachment, no user and no organisation. Every read is made with the identity of the person who called the tool, so a tool only ever shows what that person is already allowed to see in Jira Service Management.

4. Where is the data processed?

Only in the Atlassian tenant of the customer: inside the Forge runtime of the app and through the JSM Cloud REST API of the site where the app is installed. No data leaves that tenant, no service outside Atlassian is contacted.

5. How long is it retained?

Not at all. The app writes nothing, so there is nothing to export and nothing to erase. The answer returned by a tool lives only in the answer itself, which goes to the MCP client that asked for it; the app keeps no copy of it.

6. Third-party SDKs / data sharing?

None. The app has no third-party SDK, no analytics, no external API call and no cookie. Its only dependency is Atlassian's own @forge/api.

7. Access, correction, deletion

Because nothing is stored by the app, there is no personal data of yours held by the app to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the app removes its installation entirely. The data it reads remains in your Jira Service Management site and stays under your own control.

8. Consent

The first call may require you to grant the app access to Jira Service Management on your site. Until you accept, the tools answer with an explicit refusal and read nothing.
